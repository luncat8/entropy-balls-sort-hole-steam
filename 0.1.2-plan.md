# 0.1.2 — ball trajectories toward the central wall

Goal: visualize where balls heading for the central wall will hit it. For every
ball moving toward the wall, draw its path to the predicted impact point and mark
that point in the ball's color.

Parent: `0.1.0-plan.md`.

## Prediction (sim.js, per ball, per frame)

For a ball in the left chamber moving right (`vx > 0`) or in the right chamber
moving left (`vx < 0`):

```
t = (CX - x) / vx            // time to reach the wall plane
yHit = y + vy * t
```

If `yHit` lies inside the current hole interval `[holeY - holeR, holeY + holeR]`,
the ball passes through: no marker, no reflection line (it is not "toward the
wall" in the sorting sense). Otherwise it is a hit: record `yHit` clamped to
`[r, H - r]` and the ball color.

Hole motion during `t` is ignored for prediction (hole is slow relative to balls);
this is a visualization, not a guarantee.

## Render (render.js)

- For each hitting ball, draw a dashed line from `(x, y)` to `(CX, yHit)` in the
  ball's color at low alpha.
- Draw a small filled circle at `(CX, yHit)` in the ball's color.
- Accumulate recent hits (last 2 s) as persistent spots on the wall so the
  distribution is visible; fade older spots. Reuse a fixed ring buffer of
  `MAX_SPOTS` (default 256) entries `{ y, color, age }`; update `age` in place.
- Spots are cleared when the hole moves more than `holeR` in one frame (the
  wall's "target" changed), to avoid stale marks.

## Performance

Prediction and spot update run in the same ball loop as step. No new objects per
frame: write into preallocated arrays indexed by ball index; spots mutate in
place.

## Validation

- L1: spots appear where balls actually reflect; passing balls leave no spot.
- Manual pass: with `ballCollisions` off, every dashed line ends at a real bounce;
  no line is drawn for a ball moving away from the wall.
