plan 3.0 — a wall mark is drawn at the ball's size
changed: territory.js (feature: `territoryMarkWidth` — painted segments and their passage outline are one ball diameter wide, centered on the divider; the unpainted plate keeps the plate thickness)
changed: plans/plan3.0.md (feature: the mark-size rule), experiments/mode_smoke.js (feature: drawn width, centering and one-contact height)
validation: node --check territory.js experiments/mode_smoke.js; mode_smoke 63/63 (62 before)
decisions: the contact span along the wall was already one ball diameter (probe: 20/28/52 for r=10/14/26), so the missing dimension was across the plate — the band is widened, the paint state stays untouched, so passages, shares and the whole-body fit rule do not move
decisions: the band uses the mean ball radius from the ball-size setting, not the contacting ball's own radius: a segment is shared by contacts of many balls and per-contact width would need a width per segment (a second paint state) for a mark that is a cosmetic band
pitfalls: mark heights come out of the Float32Array segment store, so a smoke comparison against 2r needs a 1e-3 tolerance, not 1e-6
next step: play-test the wide band against the guns that sit on the wall and against room walls near the divider
