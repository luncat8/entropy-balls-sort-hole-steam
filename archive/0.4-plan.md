# 0.4 — Objective difficulty

## Outcome

Every objective carries a difficulty score and a label. Generation only assigns objectives inside a playable band, so a task never asks for something already true or for something the field cannot pay for, and the label spread is controlled rather than accidental.

## Design

### Score

`tasksCost(cat, cmp, thresh, isLeft)` returns the fewest balls that must change chamber for the objective to hold, or `-1` when no split of the live population satisfies it.

- A split is `(s, m)`: `s` balls on the named chamber, `m` of them in the category, bounded by `max(0, n - (nAll - s)) <= m <= min(n, s)` where `n` is the category total.
- The only way to move `(s, m)` is a ball crossing the opening, and each crossing changes it by one step, so for a fixed split the cost is the unit-L1 distance from the current counts: `|m - sc| + |(s - m) - (sn - sc)|`, with `sc` the named chamber's category count. The score is the minimum over satisfying splits.
- The threshold is one linear inequality in `m`, so the satisfying `m` for a given `s` form one run; the cost is convex and flat between `m = sc` and `m = s - u` (where `u = sn - sc`), so the cheaper end of that stretch clamped into the run is the cheapest allowed `m`. One loop over `s`, integer arithmetic, `O(nAll)`, no allocation.
- `tasksFeasible` is `tasksCost >= 0` on either chamber: reachability reads only the live population, so the named side cannot change the answer.

### Bands

The score is crossings, so it is directly comparable across objectives and populations.

| label | crossings | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `easy` | 3 – 5 | a few balls |
| `normal` | 6 – 10 | a short sequence |
| `hard` | 11 – 17 | every opening visit must be used |
| `expert` | 18 – `tasksCostMax()` | more than half of the live balls |

- `TASK_COST_MIN = 3` is the floor. At 1 or 2 crossings the outcome is decided by two balls, and balls already cross the opening at random, so such a task completes on its own while the player is doing something else.
- `tasksCostMax() = min(TASK_COST_CAP, floor(0.75 * total))`. The fraction keeps the ceiling meaningful while the field is filling from 13 to 36 balls; the constant caps it at full field.
- A score of `0` (already true) and `-1` (unreachable) sit outside every band and are never assigned, so neither a coin flip nor an impossible target can appear.
- A rare category is priced by construction. "60% of left is violet" with two violet balls on the whole canvas costs one crossing per non-violet ball shipped out plus one per violet ball still outside — the same order as the prompt's example — so it reaches `expert` instead of being offered cheaply. A named side that is only a few balls from the threshold is priced at 3–5 and stays reachable.

### Assignment

- The candidate space is unchanged: side × category × comparator × `30, 40, 50, 60, 70` × completion mode, decoded from one packed index.
- One pass costs every candidate into `tasksCostScratch` and counts the in-band ones per label. The label is then drawn uniformly from the labels that have a candidate, and a candidate is drawn uniformly inside it. A band's width therefore does not decide its own frequency.
- The threshold ladder stays as it is. Loosening a threshold only adds splits, never removes them, so the score is close to monotone in the threshold and every reachable band is already within reach at five rungs; measured in-band shares are `0.896` at five rungs, `0.888` at eleven and `0.923` at seven. Every rung is also a legal target, because any reachable `m` is achievable by positioning the aperture.
- A threshold ladder plus the floor guarantees coverage: with every rung blocked the cost of each rung is zero, and a chamber whose every category is weakly blocking is already a satisfying split at the loosest threshold. The lowest rung costs one crossing, inside `easy`. Measured: no distribution in `13..36` balls lacks an in-band candidate; only populations of four balls or fewer can, and play starts at thirteen.
- Generation cost is `O(candidates * nAll)`, about `0.24 ms` at the cap in a shared global scope, and it runs only on attach and on completion — never in the per-step path.

### Task view

The read-only view gains `cost` and `difficulty`. The strip leads with the label, e.g. `easy right hold >70% red 2/7 0/2s`, and the cost is part of the paint signature. A manually assigned objective whose cost is unreachable is labelled `impossible` rather than crashing on a missing band.

## Files and ownership

- `tasks.js` — `tasksCost`, `tasksCostMax`, `tasksBand`, `tasksInBand`, `tasksDifficulty`, the label table, the in-band filter and the label-first draw in `tasksGenerate`, difficulty in the view model and the strip.
- `experiments/difficulty_sweep.js` — node sweep over seeded distributions: cost histogram, quantiles, band histogram, the label mix actually assigned, and how many distributions have no in-band candidate at all.
- `experiments/task_smoke.js` — score, band, rare-category and label checks.
- No change to `simulation.js`, `canvas.js`, `input.js`, `players.js`, `main.js`, `index.html`: the score reads only `simDist`.

## Validation

- L0: `node --check` on touched JavaScript.
- L1: score identity against a brute-force sum of the four move types (1920 candidate/side combinations, 0 mismatches); absent category, already-met and unreachable cases; the rare-category prompt case scores at least `hard` and dearer than the common category; every generated objective is in band and not already met; both ends of the band and the population-scaled cap; the strip shows the label.
- L2: `node experiments/sim_smoke.js`, `node experiments/difficulty_sweep.js`, and a boot check that the strip renders a label without `NaN`/`undefined`.
- Manual: play several objectives to completion; the strip never shows an objective that is already true, always shows a label, and never asks for a category that barely exists on the named side.
